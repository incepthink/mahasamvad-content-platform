## Overview

We have designed and implemented an AI-assisted digital content platform for creating accurate, branded and publication-ready communication material. The system can work with typed notes, official documents, scanned PDFs, audio recordings and YouTube videos.

The following deliverables can be produced through the platform.

## About the Client

We are currently working with a major government media production and public communications department. This engagement gives us direct experience in building AI-assisted content workflows for a large public-sector organisation, where accuracy, consistency, scale and responsible handling of official information are essential.

Because the department works with sensitive data, we are also working on deploying an on-premises AI model within its controlled infrastructure. This approach is intended to give the department greater control over its information and AI systems while supporting its security, privacy and data-governance requirements.

## Content Deliverables

### 1. News Articles and Press Releases

Publication-ready Marathi news articles and press releases created from meeting notes, official documents, recordings or YouTube videos. The content is structured in a formal editorial style and remains grounded in the supplied source material.

### 2. Social Media Posters and Creatives

Branded static creatives for X, Facebook and Instagram, with layouts selected or generated according to the amount and type of information provided. Marathi text, official logos and footer elements are presented consistently.

### 3. Animated Posters and Motion Creatives

Approved static posters can be converted into short motion creatives delivered as MP4 videos and GIFs. Selected visual elements are animated while the original text, figures, icons, logos, colours and branding remain fixed and readable.

### 4. Article Banners

Professional landscape banners created for news articles, websites, digital publications and campaign pages. The banner highlights the main subject while maintaining the required visual identity.

### 5. YouTube Thumbnails

Branded and attention-focused thumbnails created from a video topic, article or supplied brief. The design is prepared in the correct YouTube format with clear Marathi messaging.

### 6. Social Media Captions

Ready-to-publish Marathi captions for social media posts. Captions can be supplied with a creative or delivered separately for use across different communication channels.

### 7. "What Changed?" Evidence-Based Carousels

A Marathi carousel that explains how a selected topic has progressed over time. It compares earlier and current published information and presents the timeline, confirmed changes, unchanged points, conflicting records and officially supported next steps, with citations to the source articles on every factual section.

### 8. Marathi Explainer Videos

Branded vertical or landscape explainer videos created from a note or approved script. The final video can include planned scenes, Marathi voiceover, on-screen key points, subtitles, official branding and a closing contact slate.

### 9. Audio and YouTube Transcripts

Marathi transcripts produced from meetings, interviews, speeches, voice notes and YouTube videos. These transcripts can be delivered independently or used as the source for an article, caption or video.

### 10. English and Hindi Translations

English and Hindi versions of Marathi articles, documents and public communication material. Important names, places, organisations, schemes and official terms are checked for consistent spelling.

### 11. Proofread and Corrected Content

Marathi or English content reviewed for spelling, grammar, punctuation and style. Corrections are clearly identified while preserving the meaning and structure of the original content.

### 12. Publication-Ready PDF Documents

Finished articles and translations formatted as professional PDF documents for official circulation, record keeping or publication.

### 13. Canva-Editable Designs

Completed poster designs prepared for further editing in Canva. Important branding elements can remain separate and protected while the main artwork stays editable.

### 14. Complete Multi-Format Content Packages

A single note, document or recording can be converted into a coordinated package containing a news article, social media poster, animated poster, article banner, YouTube thumbnail, caption, translations and an explainer video.

## Delivery Standards

All deliverables are prepared with emphasis on:

- accurate use of the supplied source material;
- correct Marathi and Devanagari presentation;
- consistent names, designations and official terminology;
- approved logos, colours and brand placement;
- editable review stages before final delivery; and
- formats suitable for websites, social media, print and video platforms.
